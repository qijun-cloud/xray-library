How to cite: please cite the original paper and figure listed with each spectrum. The curves were traced from published figures, so energies and intensities are approximate (see each spectrum's confidence level); use the original data for quantitative work. Reprint links point to the de Groot group website; no PDFs are hosted here. Download the whole dataset: spectra_library.json.

Element, technique and edge tags come from the paper titles automatically, so some are missing or approximate. They get corrected as each paper is digitized. PDF links go to the reprints on the de Groot group website.
